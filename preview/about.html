About Riaz Home Decor

Fanoos Light & Decor – A dedicated lighting and interior decor showroom in Multan.

Curated Ambient Fixtures & Contemporary Accents

Riaz Home Decor Fanoos Light & Decor is a local home decor and lighting store based in Multan, Pakistan, offering a curated selection of ambient lighting fixtures and handcrafted decor pieces for residential and architectural spaces.

Our collection brings together signature traditional fanoos luminaires, contemporary statement chandeliers, sleek pendant lights, flush-mount ceiling fixtures, ambient wall sconces, and refined table and floor lamps. Complementing our illumination gallery is a thoughtful selection of home accents, including geometric mirrors, textured ceramic vases, decorative wall art, and sculptural tabletop showpieces.

We believe lighting is the foundational element that defines an interior's character. Whether you are designing an entire residence or searching for an individual accent piece, our digital store and physical showroom provide clear specifications, dimensions, and material details to assist you in making informed decisions.

Fanoos & Lighting

Intricate filigree pendants, crystal and modern chandeliers, warm wall sconces, and task lamps.
