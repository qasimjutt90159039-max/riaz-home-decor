Shipping & Handling Policy

Careful handling, reinforced protective crating, and coordinated transport for delicate lighting fixtures and home decor accents.

1. Packaging and Fragile Care

Our lighting fixtures, chandeliers, and glass fanoos components require dedicated shock-absorbent cushioning and specialized packaging. Each order is prepared to ensure secure transit from our Multan location.

2. Delivery Arrangements & Rates

Shipping rates, estimated transit times, and carrier selections are determined based on order dimensions, volumetric weight, and destination. In the live Shopify store, exact delivery rates and regional options are displayed during checkout or configured directly in Shopify Admin.

3. Showroom Pickup

Clients in Multan and surrounding regions are welcome to select showroom pickup or coordinate local delivery directly by visiting our showroom on Bosan Road, Sabzazar Colony, Multan, or calling +92 300 7490606.
